Our verdict

The most interesting evidence story in this list. An audit tells you a system was configured correctly the day it was inspected; two federal subpoenas tell you the data did not exist when the government actually asked. What you give up for that is jurisdiction: a US-based provider lives under laws that can force cooperation. For torrenting and power use specifically, PIA remains hard to beat.

Why we scored it this way

Open-source apps, a Deloitte audit history and two genuine court tests. US 5-Eyes jurisdiction caps the privacy score because a US provider can be compelled to cooperate in ways Panama or Switzerland providers cannot. The 3-year price is the cheapest term in this list.

A note on our speed scoreWe have not run our own speed benchmarks, so we do not pretend we have. This speed score is an editorial estimate built from network architecture — protocol support, RAM-only versus disk infrastructure, server density and dedicated streaming capacity — together with findings from published independent reviews. It is a reliable indicator of which providers are fast in general. It is not a measurement of your line, your ISP or your home.

How much does Private Internet Access cost?

The cheapest plan we list works out at $1.64 per month for 3 years, which is $59 in total. Can Private Internet Access really be trusted not to log my data?

The claim is supported by 3 independent audit engagements with Deloitte, the most recent dated 2026-01. RAM-only servers: Yes. Intelligence alliance membership: Inside the alliance.
