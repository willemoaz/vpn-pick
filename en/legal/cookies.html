Cookie policy

Our own list is short: one preference cookie, optional analytics, and local storage. Advertising is served by Google AdSense under Google's own policy, described in its own section below.

Last updated: October 8, 2026

What we actually set

By default this site sets one cookie, and only if you interact with the consent banner: a first-party preference cookie that records your choice so the banner does not reappear. It contains no identifier, is not readable by any other site, and expires after twelve months.

Analytics is Google Analytics, gated by the consent banner: until you accept it runs consent-denied and sets no cookies; after accept it may set its usual measurement cookies (_ga and similar). Advertising cookies come from Google AdSense, described below — not from us.

What we never set

  • No advertising cookies set by us — Google's are described below.
  • No third-party tracking pixels, beacons or social pixels of our own.
  • No cross-site or cross-domain tracking cookies of our own.
  • No device or browser fingerprinting.
  • No data sold, rented or shared by us with advertising networks.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

Pages on this site load Google AdSense, which serves the display advertising that funds the free comparison. Google acts as an independent controller for the data it processes for that purpose — we do not control, access or receive it.

Google may use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads, including personalised advertising where you have consented. Without consent, Google serves non-personalised ads. Read how Google uses data from partner sites, manage ad personalisation in Google Ad Settings, and read its advertising principles: how Google uses partner-site data · Google Ad Settings · Google's advertising principles.

Blocking Google's advertising cookies does not break this site: the comparison, prices and reviews work the same with or without them.

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage for one purpose: remembering whether you chose a light or dark theme, and which order you last sorted the comparison table in. This never leaves your device and is not transmitted to us.

Cookies set by providers, not by us

When you click an affiliate link and arrive at a VPN provider's site, that provider may set its own cookies, including an affiliate tracking cookie that credits the referral if you subscribe. Those cookies are governed by the provider's privacy policy and its consent regime, not by this one. We have no access to them and no control over them.

How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may mean your theme choice and consent answer are not remembered between visits. Most browsers also offer a built-in tracking protection mode.
